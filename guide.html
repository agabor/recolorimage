Welcome to Recolor Image!

Hey there! Welcome to our Recolor Image tool. This handy online tool helps you transform your photos to perfectly match your website's color palette in just a few clicks. No more mismatched images disrupting your site's visual harmony!

Why Match Images to Your Website Colors?

Having images that match your website's color scheme creates a professional, cohesive look that:

  • Strengthens your brand identity
  • Creates visual harmony across your entire site
  • Makes your content look professionally designed
  • Improves user experience with consistent visuals
  • Helps your website stand out from competitors

How to Use the Recolor Image Tool

Step 1: Upload Your Image

Ready to transform your images? Here's how to get started:

  • Drag and drop your image into the upload area, or click to select a file from your computer
  • We support JPG and PNG formats
  • Your image stays on your device - we don't store or upload it to any servers
  • Once loaded, you'll see a preview of your image

Step 2: Choose Your Color Palette

Now for the important part! Choose a color palette that matches your website:

  • We offer several pre-defined palettes that work with popular website themes
  • For the best results, create a custom palette using your exact website colors
  • Use your website's color codes (hex values like #FF5500) for perfect matching
  • Include both your primary brand colors and complementary accent colors

Your palette has two important parts:

  • Luminance Palette: These colors handle the light and dark areas of your image (from shadows to highlights)
  • Hue Palette: These colors determine the actual color tones in your image

Step 3: Adjust Your Settings (Optional)

Want more control? You can tweak these settings:

  • Use the color count slider to limit how many colors appear in your final image
  • Advanced users can adjust thresholds for more precise control (but the defaults work great for most images!)

Step 4: Recolor Your Image

Ready to see the magic happen? Just click the "Recolor" button! Your image will be processed right in your browser - no uploading to external servers or waiting for email results.

Step 5: Save Your Creation

Love what you see? Click the download button to save your recolored image as a PNG file. It's that simple!

Tips for Website-Perfect Images

  • Use your exact website colors: For the best match, create a custom palette using your website's exact color codes (find these in your theme settings or using a color picker browser extension)
  • Balance brand recognition with aesthetics: Include your main brand colors but also add complementary shades for depth
  • Maintain contrast: Make sure your palette includes both light and dark colors for proper image definition
  • Process multiple images with the same palette: Create a consistent look across all your website images
  • Consider image placement: Think about where the image will appear on your site and which colors will surround it

Website Integration Tips

Once you've recolored your images to match your website:

  • Use consistent image sizes: Resize your recolored images to fit your website's layout
  • Apply the same treatment to all images: Process all your website images with the same palette for consistency
  • Consider image context: Make sure the recolored images work well with surrounding text and elements
  • Save in the right format: PNG works best for graphics with solid colors, while JPG is better for photographs
  • Optimize file sizes: Compress your recolored images before uploading to your website for faster loading
